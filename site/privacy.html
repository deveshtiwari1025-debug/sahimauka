Privacy Policy — SahiMauka

Effective: 2026-10-31 · This app collects and transmits NO personal data to any server.

What the app does

SahiMauka displays verified student opportunities (scholarships, hackathons, exams, government schemes) from official public sources: the Devpost official API and the Press Information Bureau (Government of India) RSS feed. Items link to their official pages.

Data the app accesses

  • Nothing about you leaves your device. There is no sign-up, no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK.
  • Tracked categories and reminders are stored only on your device (app-local storage) so deadline notifications can fire. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • Network requests the app makes: downloading the public feed JSON from deveshtiwari1025-debug.github.io (no identifiers sent), and opening official opportunity links in your browser.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally by the app itself.

Data we share

None. There is no backend that receives user data, and no third-party data sharing.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for students and collects no personal information from any user, including children under 13.

Changes

Material changes will ship with app updates and update the effective date above.
