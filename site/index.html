How it works

  1. Ingestion pulls from official APIs/RSS on a schedule (Devpost official API, PIB Govt. of India RSS).
  2. Every item is verified: source-domain allowlist, deadline sanity, pay-to-apply scam flags.
  3. Flagged or expired items never render. A failing source contributes nothing — honesty over vanity.
  4. The app adds what boards don't: deadline countdowns, reminders, and a first-run tour.

Business model

Free forever for students. SahiMauka Plus (₹49/mo, ₹299/yr) for unlimited trackers and personalised alerts. Colleges and employers pay per verified posting (₹499/post) with source attestation. Details in BUSINESS_MODEL.md.
