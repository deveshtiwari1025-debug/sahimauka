How it works

Applying to a C-DAC internship

  1. 1
    Register as a student

    Create your AICTE Internship Portal account and verify your email.

  2. 2
    Complete your profile

    Add your institute, course and skills — IO centres shortlist from your profile.

  3. 3
    Apply to an IO posting

    Pick a posting from C-DAC, NIELIT, ERNET or any other implementing organisation and apply before its deadline.

  4. 4
    Complete & get certified

    Your internship moves from ongoing to completed on this PMU dashboard once the centre signs off.
